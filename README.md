# Delvora 3D

A free block game that runs right in your browser. Explore, build and survive across 12 quests, solo or with friends in an online room.

**Play:** https://kaleidoscopic-pegasus-0de9d3.netlify.app

## Features

- 3D voxel world with a forest, a frozen north and a lava south
- Mining, building and a crafting bench (wood to magma tools, ice coat, basalt boots)
- Monsters in every biome: zombies, snow wolves, ice and basalt golems, slimes
- 12 quests that lead to the Magma pickaxe
- Online rooms with chat and voice (peer to peer, up to 8 players)
- God powers for the host: fly, invisible, big brush, undo, time control
- Works on phones and computers, and offline in solo mode (PWA)

## Controls

- **Phone:** drag on the left half to move, drag on the right half to look. Hold Mine, tap Place.
- **Computer:** click the game to look around. WASD moves, Space jumps, left click mines, right click places, 1–9 change block, C opens Craft, M opens the map.
